# Backlog

Features to consider after version 1.

1. **Sri Yantra center:** 9 interlocking triangles. The exact construction requires solving for line positions so that several lines meet at single points.
2. **Metatron's Cube center:** the 13 circles of the Fruit of Life connected by straight lines.
3. **Outer square gate (bhupura):** the square frame with 4 T-shaped gates found around traditional yantras.
4. **Color mode:** fills with palettes, for example chakra colors.
5. **Share link:** store all parameters in the URL so a mandala can be bookmarked or sent.
6. **Musical ratios:** spirograph (hypotrochoid) rings with ratios from musical intervals such as 3:2 and 4:3.
7. **Print layout:** export to PDF at letter and A4 sizes with margins.
8. **Animation:** removed from version 1 by request.
